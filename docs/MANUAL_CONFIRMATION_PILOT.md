# Manual-confirmation payment pilot — working plan

**Decision:** Customers pay independent milk sellers directly by UPI or COD. Sellers pay Doodhwala a separate monthly charge. This is a manual-confirmation pilot, not an automated payment gateway or bank-settlement service.

**Status:** Work in progress on a development branch. No migration, frontend or seller-billing implementation is authorized for production by this document. There has been no staging end-to-end, physical-device, compliance, security or real-money sign-off. Do not collect live payments until the owner and relevant reviewers approve the gates below.

## Two separate payments

| Transaction | Payee | Who checks their own account or cash? | What the application can say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Milk order, direct UPI | The selected seller | That order's authorized seller | **Seller-confirmed** after the seller checks their own bank/UPI account. The seller's reference and attestation are not independent verification by Doodhwala. |
| Milk order, COD | The selected seller or their authorized delivery person | The authorized seller confirms collection after delivery | **Seller-confirmed cash collection**, not a Doodhwala receipt. |
| Seller's monthly charge | Doodhwala's business account | An authorized Doodhwala admin | **Admin-confirmed** only after the admin reconciles Doodhwala's business account and the correct invoice. This is not independent bank verification by the application. |

The order payment and the seller's monthly invoice must use separate records, amounts, payees, permissions and audit trails. Paying one must never settle the other. No order commission or automatic fee deduction is part of this model. A QR code, opening or returning from a UPI app, a customer statement, screenshot or entered reference does not establish settlement. Never request a UPI PIN, OTP, password or card security code.

## Owner-selected seller plans

| Plan | Monthly price | Included |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Normal listing | ₹2,000 | The seller's name/listing in Doodhwala. |
| Recommended + advertising | ₹3,500 **total** | The normal listing plus a recommended tag and in-app advertising. |

A seller chooses one plan for a billing period. The premium plan is not an additional ₹3,500 on top of the normal plan. The owner wants a text-only **“Recommended by Doodhwala”** label. The initial proposed in-app promotion is on the seller's existing discovery card: display **“Recommended by Doodhwala · Sponsored”**, plus a short neutral line such as “View milk, prices and delivery details” near the existing store link. The adjacent Sponsored disclosure identifies paid promotion rather than suggesting an independent endorsement. Do not add a banner, carousel or hidden paid ranking for this initial design; normal search and sort rules remain unchanged. Campaign limits, duration, billing and other commercial terms still need agreement. Neither plan, the tag, nor any advertising campaign is live or activated by this document.

## Operating procedure after launch approval

### Customer paying the seller

1. Show the seller, item quantity, amount, delivery details and payment method before the customer confirms. COD and UPI are separate choices.
2. For UPI, open the order and confirm that the displayed payee and amount correspond to the intended seller and order. The customer authorizes payment in their own UPI app; Doodhwala never asks for their PIN or OTP.
3. The customer checks their own app for the transaction result. They must not pay again merely because Doodhwala still shows a pending status. Contact the seller/support if the result is unclear.
4. The order remains pending verification until its seller checks their own bank/UPI account and explicitly confirms through the authorized seller workflow. The system's label describes a seller attestation, not independent proof of bank settlement.
5. If a checkout response is uncertain, check Orders before retrying. The retry must reuse the saved request key. Checkout uses an origin-wide browser lock to prevent two current-version tabs submitting at once; unsupported browsers fail closed. This lock does not cover different devices, browser profiles or stale clients. If browser storage is unavailable or damaged, or the payment method, address or cart changed and recovery is blocked, support investigates the existing order first; never advise blindly submitting a fresh order.

### Seller confirming customer payment

1. Sign in as the authorized seller and open the seller's own order. Check the incoming transaction in the seller's bank/UPI account against the expected payee, order amount and transaction details. A customer screenshot or UPI-app return is not enough.
2. If the seller can identify the receipt, use the seller-only confirmation action and record the permitted transaction reference. Do not confirm another seller's order. A reference is for reconciliation and duplicate-reference protection, not independent verification.
3. If no matching receipt is visible, leave the order pending and follow the agreed support/escalation procedure. If a refund, reversal, duplicate, wrong payee or mismatch is reported, preserve the order and audit history and escalate; do not silently overwrite status or ask the customer to pay a second time.
4. For COD, confirm collection only after delivery and actual collection, using the authorized seller action. Delivery alone must not mark cash paid.

### Doodhwala reconciling the seller's monthly charge

1. Once PR #71 or its reviewed successor has been integrated and staged, the seller receives the correct, separate invoice and the approved Doodhwala payee/payment instructions. Never substitute a milk seller's UPI ID for Doodhwala's business account.
2. The seller pays Doodhwala, not a milk customer. A submitted reference or screenshot is a reconciliation aid, not proof of payment.
3. An authorized Doodhwala admin checks Doodhwala's business account, matches payee, invoice, amount and reference, and confirms through the reviewed admin-only flow. Record who confirmed and when. The seller cannot self-confirm the invoice; the admin must not mark the customer's milk order paid.
4. Investigate wrong amounts, duplicates, refunds and reversals using an approved, audited process. Suspension, grace periods or access restrictions must not be invented; apply only owner-approved, disclosed terms without silently disrupting accepted orders.

## Staging and release gates

1. **Confirm isolated staging:** the owner confirms whether the visible `Doodhwala Staging` project is the authorized non-production target, along with its URL, authentication redirects, synthetic customer/seller/admin accounts, fake UPI IDs, test window, costs and backup/restore owner. Its existence and healthy status do not authorize test writes. Never create synthetic records in production or transfer money for these tests.
2. **Reconcile the target first:** capture its actual schema, migration history, function definitions/owners, grants and role memberships. The 2026-10-24 production history snapshot is historical and shows substantial drift; it is not a current schema or authorization audit. Verify the latest order creator, payment-choice/readiness guards, audit triggers, RLS and all relevant tables are present and match the reviewed contract.
3. **Audit every creation path:** the proposed migration revokes named legacy/promo overloads for `PUBLIC`, `anon`, `authenticated` and `service_role`, and fails if those roles still inherit an execute grant on those names. That does **not** discover every differently named wrapper, table-write grant, security-definer function or scheduled job. Review those paths, role inheritance and exposed schemas in the actual target. Audit service-role integrations and scheduled jobs before revoking anything: callers that relied on old grants can break. The migration also checks that its definer can execute its dependencies; confirm actual ownership and least privilege.
4. **Review coordinated deployment and rollback:** old cached/open clients call legacy RPCs that the migration revokes; the new client needs the new RPC. Deploying either independently can pause checkout or leave stale clients broken. Plan an explicit maintenance window, a verified cache/open-tab update strategy, rollback ordering, backups and support communication. Do not re-enable an unsafe legacy/promo path to make stale clients work. Rehearse restore and rollback in staging without deleting order or audit records; verify what happens to in-flight requests and partial multi-seller carts.
5. **Exercise backend and UI together:** with synthetic data, test COD and UPI, two customers and two sellers, wrong-owner attempts, server-derived amount, unconfigured seller UPI, invalid or changed payment choice, lost responses and duplicate/concurrent retries, status transitions, cancelled orders, payment-reference reuse, audit events, seller confirmation and seller-billing ledger isolation. Confirm that a bank transfer is never inferred from the browser. Check every stale-client and scheduled-job scenario separately.
6. **Test real client conditions:** authenticated mobile and desktop browsers, old installed PWA/open tab, slow/offline/reconnect, storage denied/full, multi-seller partial success, device UPI-app handoff with fake/test instructions, and accurate support messaging. Record build/version, environment, steps, results and redacted evidence. A synthetic browser or SQL fixture is useful but not a substitute for actual Supabase RLS, integrations or physical devices.
7. **Seller plans and sponsored promotion:** integrate and review the separate billing work; resolve the invoice plan-conflict issue and display the actual invoice snapshot after every retry. Confirm campaign limits and plan-change/refund/expiry rules for the chosen text-only seller-card promotion. Review and harden the default-off eligibility/expiry draft, then test payment reversals and seller approval/serviceability in an authorized environment; never grant a tag or ad solely from a browser-selected plan or a submitted UPI reference. Show a clear Sponsored disclosure on paid placements. The benefit is not active or approved for launch.
8. **Operational and compliance review:** approve seller identity and ownership checks, service/support contacts, cancellation and dispute/reversal handling, business account and bank/UPI terms, monthly pricing/taxes/invoices, privacy and retention practices, food-safety/consumer requirements and any applicable payment regulation with qualified professionals. The model does not by itself establish regulatory classification or zero total cost.
9. **Explicit go/no-go:** the owner, technical/security reviewer and appropriate legal/compliance reviewers sign off the staging evidence, deployment/rollback plan and support ownership. Until then, this remains an engineering preparation, not a live-money pilot.
