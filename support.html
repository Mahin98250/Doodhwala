QUICK ANSWERS

What can we help with?

SELF-SERVICE
Where is my order?

Open My Orders after signing in. The order page shows the latest status available from the marketplace. If it looks stuck, submit a delivery request below and include the order ID.

My milk delivery is late or missing.

Check the order status first, then create a “Delivery issue” request. Include the delivery date and what happened. Do not submit sensitive payment credentials.

How do I pay for an order?

Available methods depend on the seller and checkout. Direct UPI payments go to the independent seller; the seller must verify receipt in their own bank/UPI app. A UPI app returning to the website is not proof of payment. Cash on delivery is confirmed after delivery.

How do recurring milk plans work?

Open Milk Plans to review the plans available to your account. Check the plan schedule and seller details before relying on a recurring delivery.

I'm a milk seller. How do I get help?

Sign in with the account used for your seller profile and describe the issue below. Include an order ID if the issue concerns a specific order. Never include your password, OTP, UPI PIN or bank login.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Open My Orders and check the actions available for that order. Options depend on its current status; support cannot promise a refund or cancellation before the order is reviewed.
