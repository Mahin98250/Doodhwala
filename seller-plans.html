How the payments work

We are preparing a manual-confirmation pilot, not automatic bank verification.

01

Your customer pays you

Milk-order payments go directly to the seller. Only the seller confirms receipt after checking their own account.

02

Your seller fee is separate

If billing is enabled later, Doodhwala issues a seller invoice. Opening a UPI app or sharing a reference does not confirm payment.

03

A person checks the fee

An authorized Doodhwala admin checks the receiving account and records the fee manually. Never share a UPI PIN or OTP.
